HR Analytics: Unlocking Insights from Employee Feedback Data 

Employee satisfaction directly impacts organizational productivity, retention, and overall business performance. In large organizations, understanding employee perception across 
demographics such as location, gender, tenure, and career level is crucial for data-driven HR  strategy. 
This dataset is important because: 
   It captures employee ratings across key HR dimensions: 
      o Overall Rating 
      o Career Growth 
      o Compensation & Benefits 
      o Work-Life Balance 
 It includes demographic and tenure information. 
 It contains qualitative feedback (comments), enabling text analytics. 


Motivation: 
     Identify dissatisfaction factord in companies. 
     Understand demographic influence on satisfaction. 
     Detect patterns among high-tenured vs Covid joiners. 
     Extract actionable insights from employee comments. 
This study helps HR leadership: 
     Improve retention strategy 
     Enhance employee engagement 
     Optimize career development policies 
Dataset Description 
  Dataset Overview 
       Rows: 25,866 
       Columns: 10 
       Format: Excel file 
  Source / Creation Method 
      The dataset is an internal HR employee feedback dataset collected through employee surveys. 
      It includes structured survey ratings and unstructured textual feedback.
